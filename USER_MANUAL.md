## Overview

### What is Shukran Admin Panel?

Shukran Admin Panel is a web-based management system that helps you run your e-commerce business. Think of it as your control center where you can:

- Manage products and packages
- Process customer orders
- Handle customer support tickets
- View sales reports
- Manage users and their permissions
- Send notifications to customers
- Track deliveries and returns
- Manage promotions and coupons

### Who Can Use This System?

This system is designed for:

- **Administrators** - Full access to all features
- **Managers** - Access to specific departments
- **Support Staff** - Limited access to customer support features
- **Delivery Personnel** - Access to delivery management

### System Requirements

To use the Shukran Admin Panel, you need:

- A computer, tablet, or smartphone
- An internet connection
- A modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge)
- Login credentials provided by your system administrator

## Getting Started

### Accessing the System

1. Open your web browser
2. Type in the Shukran Admin Panel website address (URL) provided by your administrator
3. You will see the login page

### Your First Time

When you first receive your login details:

1. Check your email for welcome message with login credentials
2. Make note of your username and temporary password
3. You may be asked to change your password on first login
4. Keep your new password secure and don't share it with anyone

## Login

### How to Login

1. **Open the Login Page**

   - Go to the Shukran Admin Panel website
   - You'll see the login screen

2. **Enter Your Credentials**

   - Type your username in the "Username" field
   - Type your password in the "Password" field
   - Make sure there are no extra spaces

3. **Click the Login Button**

   - Click "Login" or press Enter on your keyboard
   - Wait a few seconds while the system verifies your information

4. **Access Granted**
   - If successful, you'll be taken to the Dashboard
   - If there's an error, check your username and password and try again

### Forgot Your Password?

If you can't remember your password:

1. Click on "Forgot Password?" link on the login page
2. Enter your registered email address
3. Click "Send Reset Link"
4. Check your email inbox for a password reset link
5. Click the link in the email
6. Create a new password
7. Use your new password to login

**Password Tips:**

- Use a mix of letters, numbers, and special characters
- Make it at least 8 characters long
- Don't use obvious passwords like "12345678" or "password"
- Don't share your password with anyone

### Can't Login?

If you're having trouble logging in:

- Make sure Caps Lock is off
- Check that you're typing your username correctly
- Verify your internet connection is working
- Clear your browser's cache and cookies
- Contact your system administrator for help

## Main Features

### 1. Order Management

**What You Can Do:**

- View all customer orders
- Check order details
- Update order status
- Process refunds
- Track deliveries

**How to View Orders:**

1. Click "Orders" in the sidebar menu
2. You'll see a list of all orders
3. Use filters to find specific orders (by date, status, customer)

**How to Process an Order:**

1. Find the order in the list
2. Click on the order to open details
3. Review customer information and items ordered
4. Update the status:
   - **Pending:** New order received
   - **Processing:** Order is being prepared
   - **Shipped:** Order sent to customer
   - **Delivered:** Customer received the order
   - **Cancelled:** Order was cancelled
5. Click "Save" to update

**Order Actions:**

- **View Details:** See complete order information
- **Print Invoice:** Print receipt for customer
- **Track Delivery:** Check where the order is
- **Contact Customer:** Send message or call customer

### 2. Product Management

**What You Can Do:**

- Add new products
- Edit existing products
- Remove products
- Manage product images
- Set prices and discounts
- Control stock levels

**How to Add a New Product:**

1. Click "Products" in the sidebar
2. Click the "Add Product" or "+" button
3. Fill in product information:
   - **Product Name:** What the product is called
   - **Category:** Type of product (electronics, clothing, etc.)
   - **Brand:** Product manufacturer
   - **Description:** Details about the product
   - **Price:** How much it costs
   - **Stock Quantity:** How many you have
4. Upload product images (photos)
5. Click "Save" to add the product

**How to Edit a Product:**

1. Go to Products page
2. Find the product you want to change
3. Click the "Edit" button (pencil icon)
4. Make your changes
5. Click "Save"

**How to Remove a Product:**

1. Find the product in the list
2. Click the "Delete" button (trash icon)
3. Confirm that you want to delete it
4. The product is now removed

**Product Tips:**

- Use clear, high-quality images
- Write detailed descriptions
- Update stock quantities regularly
- Set competitive prices

### 3. Package Management

**What are Packages?**
Packages are bundles of products sold together at a special price (like gift sets or combo deals).

**How to Create a Package:**

1. Click "Packages" in the sidebar
2. Click "Create Package"
3. Enter package details:
   - Package name
   - Description
   - Price
4. Select products to include
5. Upload package image
6. Set availability and duration
7. Click "Save"

**Managing Packages:**

- View all packages
- Edit package details
- Activate or deactivate packages
- View package performance

### 4. User Management

**What You Can Do:**

- View all users (customers and staff)
- Add new users
- Edit user information
- Manage user roles and permissions
- Deactivate accounts

**How to View Users:**

1. Click "Users" in the sidebar
2. See list of all users
3. Filter by user type (customer, admin, staff)

**How to Add a New User:**

1. Go to Users page
2. Click "Add User"
3. Fill in user information:
   - Full name
   - Email address
   - Phone number
   - User role (what they can do)
   - Username and password
4. Click "Save"

**User Roles Explained:**

- **Super Admin:** Can do everything
- **Admin:** Manage most features
- **Manager:** Manage specific departments
- **Support:** Handle customer inquiries
- **Delivery:** Manage deliveries only

### 5. Delivery Management

**What You Can Do:**

- Assign orders to delivery personnel
- Track delivery status
- View delivery routes
- Manage coverage areas

**How to Assign a Delivery:**

1. Go to "Delivery" section
2. Find pending deliveries
3. Click "Assign" on an order
4. Select delivery person
5. Click "Confirm"

**Delivery Statuses:**

- **Pending:** Waiting to be assigned
- **Assigned:** Driver has the order
- - **Out for Delivery:** Driver is delivering
- **Delivered:** Successfully completed
- **Failed:** Could not deliver (customer not home, wrong address)

### 6. Reports and Analytics

**What You Can See:**

- Sales reports (daily, weekly, monthly)
- Product performance
- Customer analytics
- Transaction history
- Donation reports
- Coin usage reports

**How to View Reports:**

1. Click "Reports" in the sidebar
2. Choose the type of report you want:
   - Transaction Report
   - Package Sales Report
   - Regular Sales Report
   - Package Orders Report
   - Regular Orders Report
   - Donation Report
   - Coin Report
3. Select date range
4. Click "Generate Report"
5. View or download the report

**Understanding Reports:**

- **Graphs:** Visual representation of data
- **Tables:** Detailed numbers and figures
- **Export:** Download as PDF or Excel for printing

### 7. Coupon Management

**What You Can Do:**

- Create discount coupons
- Set coupon rules
- Track coupon usage
- Deactivate expired coupons

**How to Create a Coupon:**

1. Go to "Coupons" section
2. Click "Create Coupon"
3. Enter coupon details:
   - Coupon code (e.g., SAVE20)
   - Discount type (percentage or fixed amount)
   - Discount value
   - Valid from/to dates
   - Usage limit
   - Minimum order amount
4. Click "Save"

**Coupon Types:**

- **Percentage Discount:** 10% off, 20% off, etc.
- **Fixed Amount:** $5 off, $10 off, etc.
- **Free Shipping:** No delivery charge

### 8. Support Tickets

**What You Can Do:**

- View customer inquiries
- Respond to support tickets
- Mark tickets as resolved
- Escalate urgent issues

**How to Handle Support Tickets:**

1. Click "Support" in the sidebar
2. See list of all tickets
3. Click on a ticket to open it
4. Read customer's issue
5. Type your response
6. Click "Send Reply"
7. Mark as "Resolved" when done

**Support Priority Levels:**

- **High:** Urgent issues (payment problems, wrong orders)
- **Medium:** General questions
- **Low:** Information requests

### 9. Notifications

**What You Can Do:**

- Send notifications to customers
- Broadcast announcements
- Schedule notifications
- View notification history

**How to Send a Notification:**

1. Go to "Notifications"
2. Click "Create Notification"
3. Choose recipients:
   - All users
   - Specific user group
   - Individual users
4. Write your message:
   - Title
   - Message content
5. Add images if needed
6. Choose to send now or schedule for later
7. Click "Send"

**Notification Best Practices:**

- Keep messages short and clear
- Use friendly language
- Include important information
- Don't send too many notifications

### 10. Settings

**What You Can Manage:**

- System settings
- Payment methods
- Shipping options
- Email templates
- Coverage areas
- Package settings

**How to Access Settings:**

1. Click "Settings" in the sidebar
2. Choose the setting category you want to change
3. Make your changes
4. Click "Save"

**Important Settings:**

- **Business Information:** Company name, address, contact
- **Currency:** What currency to use
- **Tax Settings:** Tax rates and rules
- **Email Settings:** Automated email configurations

### 11. Content Management

**What You Can Do:**

- Manage website banners
- Update promotional content
- Edit homepage content
- Manage categories

**How to Update Content:**

1. Go to "Content Management"
2. Select what you want to edit
3. Make changes (text, images, layout)
4. Preview your changes
5. Click "Publish"

### 12. Coin Management

**What is Coin System?**
Digital coins that customers earn and can use for purchases (like loyalty points).

**What You Can Do:**

- Set coin earning rules
- Manage coin redemption
- View coin transactions
- Award bonus coins

**Managing Coins:**

1. Go to "Coin Management"
2. View coin settings and rules
3. See customer coin balances
4. Manually award or deduct coins if needed

### 13. Referrals

**What is Referral System?**
Customers earn rewards for inviting friends to use the platform.

**What You Can Do:**

- View referral statistics
- Manage referral rewards
- Track successful referrals

### 14. Donations

**What You Can Do:**

- View donation transactions
- Manage donation channels
- Generate donation reports

### 15. Role Management

**What You Can Do:**

- Create user roles
- Set permissions for each role
- Assign roles to users

**Creating a Role:**

1. Go to "Role Management"
2. Click "Create Role"
3. Name the role
4. Select permissions (what this role can do)
5. Click "Save"

**Permission Types:**

- **View:** Can see information
- **Create:** Can add new items
- **Edit:** Can change existing items
- **Delete:** Can remove items
- **Full Access:** Can do everything

## Common Issues

### Problem: Can't Login

**Possible Solutions:**

- Check username and password spelling
- Make sure Caps Lock is off
- Clear browser cache
- Try a different browser
- Reset your password
- Contact administrator

### Problem: Page Not Loading

**Possible Solutions:**

- Check internet connection
- Refresh the page (press F5)
- Clear browser cache and cookies
- Try different browser
- Wait a few minutes and try again

### Problem: Can't Upload Images

**Possible Solutions:**

- Check image file size (should be under 5MB)
- Use supported formats (JPG, PNG)
- Check internet connection
- Try uploading one image at a time
- Compress large images before uploading

### Problem: Changes Not Saving

**Possible Solutions:**

- Make sure all required fields are filled
- Check internet connection
- Don't click "Save" multiple times
- Wait for confirmation message
- Refresh page and try again

### Problem: Can't See Certain Menu Items

**Possible Solutions:**

- You might not have permission
- Contact your administrator
- Check if you're logged in with correct account

### Problem: Search Not Working

**Possible Solutions:**

- Check spelling
- Try different search terms
- Use broader search terms
- Clear filters if applied
- Refresh the page

### Problem: Report Not Generating

**Possible Solutions:**

- Check if date range is valid
- Make sure there's data for selected period
- Try smaller date range
- Wait a few moments and try again
- Try downloading instead of viewing

### Problem: Notification Not Sent

**Possible Solutions:**

- Check recipients are selected
- Verify message content is complete
- Check internet connection
- Try sending to smaller group first
- Check notification history

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### General Questions

**Q: How do I change my password?**
A: Click on your profile icon (top right) → Settings → Security → Change Password

**Q: How do I logout?**
A: Click on your profile icon (top right) → Logout

**Q: Can I use this on my phone?**
A: Yes, the system works on smartphones, tablets, and computers.

**Q: What browsers are supported?**
A: Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge (latest versions recommended).

**Q: How often is data updated?**
A: Most data updates in real-time. Reports may take a few minutes to generate.

### Orders

**Q: How do I cancel an order?**
A: Open the order → Click "Actions" → Select "Cancel Order" → Confirm

**Q: Can I edit an order after it's placed?**
A: You can modify orders in "Pending" or "Processing" status. Shipped orders cannot be edited.

**Q: How do I print an invoice?**
A: Open the order → Click "Print Invoice" button

**Q: What if customer address is wrong?**
A: Open the order → Edit → Update delivery address → Save

### Products

**Q: How many images can I upload per product?**
A: Usually 5-10 images per product (check with administrator for exact limit).

**Q: What image size is recommended?**
A: Use images at least 800x800 pixels for best quality.

**Q: Can I bulk upload products?**
A: This depends on your system configuration. Contact administrator for bulk upload options.

**Q: How do I manage product variants (sizes, colors)?**
A: When creating/editing product, look for "Variants" section to add different options.

### Users

**Q: How do I deactivate a user?**
A: Go to Users → Find user → Click "Deactivate" or "Block" button

**Q: Can users have multiple roles?**
A: Usually one role per user. Contact administrator if you need custom permissions.

**Q: How do I reset a user's password?**
A: Go to Users → Select user → Click "Reset Password" → System sends reset link to user's email

### Delivery

**Q: How do I track a delivery?**
A: Go to Orders → Open order → Click "Track Delivery" to see current location

**Q: What if delivery fails?**
A: Mark delivery as "Failed" → Add reason → Contact customer to reschedule

**Q: Can I reassign a delivery to different driver?**
A: Yes, in Delivery section → Find delivery → Click "Reassign" → Select new driver

### Reports

**Q: Can I schedule automatic reports?**
A: This depends on your system. Check Settings → Reports for scheduling options.

**Q: What format can I download reports in?**
A: Usually PDF and Excel (CSV) formats are available.

**Q: How far back can I view historical data?**
A: Usually all historical data is available. Very large date ranges may take longer to load.

### Coupons

**Q: Can customers use multiple coupons?**
A: Usually only one coupon per order. This is configurable in coupon settings.

**Q: Why isn't my coupon working?**
A: Check: expiry date, usage limit reached, minimum order requirement, active status

**Q: How do I stop a coupon immediately?**
A: Go to Coupons → Find coupon → Click "Deactivate"

### Notifications

**Q: Do customers need to have app installed to receive notifications?**
A: Depends on notification type - emails work without app, push notifications need app.

**Q: Can I see who read my notification?**
A: Check notification history for delivery and read status.

**Q: Can I edit a notification after sending?**
A: No, sent notifications cannot be edited. You can send a new notification with corrections.

### Technical Issues

**Q: Why is the system slow?**
A: Could be slow internet, high traffic, or large data being loaded. Try refreshing or come back later.

**Q: What if I accidentally deleted something?**
A: Contact administrator immediately. Some deletions can be reversed.

**Q: Can I undo my last action?**
A: Most actions require confirmation. Once confirmed, contact administrator for help undoing.

**Q: Is my data backed up?**
A: Yes, the system automatically backs up data regularly.

### Support

**Q: Who do I contact for help?**
A: Contact your system administrator or IT support team.

**Q: Is there training available?**
A: Ask your administrator about training sessions or video tutorials.

**Q: Can I suggest new features?**
A: Yes! Contact your administrator with your suggestions.

**Q: What if I find a bug?**
A: Report it to your administrator with details: what you were doing, what happened, screenshots if possible.
